Tenant Insurance

Not just a “landlord requirement”—it helps protect your belongings and liability exposure. Learn what it covers and what to check before buying.

Renting Guide Move-out Cleaning Metro Vancouver

Typical Coverage Areas

  • Contents: electronics, furniture, clothing
  • Liability: third‑party damage/injury caused by accidents
  • Additional living expenses: temporary accommodation in some cases
  • Deductible: lower deductible usually means higher premium

Common Mistakes

  • Assuming landlord insurance covers tenant belongings
  • Buying without checking liability limits
  • Not declaring high‑value items properly
  • Waiting until the last minute before move‑in
