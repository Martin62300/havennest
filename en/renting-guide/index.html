Renting Guide (Metro Vancouver)

A practical checklist for searching, viewing, applying, signing, and moving in—so you can rent faster and with fewer surprises.

Tenant Insurance Move-out Cleaning Listings Map

Suggested Steps

  • Set budget and commute targets (work/school)
  • Filter by unit type and must-haves (parking, pets, furnished)
  • Viewing checklist: light, noise, appliances, building rules
  • Prepare documents early (income proof, references, credit)
  • Review lease terms: utilities, deposits, move-in/out fees
  • Move-in inspection: take photos and keep records

Avoid Common Pitfalls

  • Never send deposits before verifying and viewing
  • Confirm what’s included (water/gas/parking/storage)
  • Ask about building move-in/out rules and fees
  • Clarify maintenance responsibilities and timelines
