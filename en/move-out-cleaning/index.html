Move-out Cleaning

A clean move‑out reduces deposit disputes. Use a checklist, document the condition, and confirm what the landlord/strata expects.

Renting Guide Tenant Insurance Metro Vancouver

High‑impact Areas

  • Kitchen: stove/oven, fridge, range hood grease
  • Bathrooms: limescale, mold, drains, mirrors
  • Floors: vacuum/mop, carpet spot cleaning
  • Windows & tracks, doors, baseboards

Move‑out Tips

  • Take photos/videos before handing over keys
  • Confirm check‑out inspection time in advance
  • Keep receipts if hiring professional cleaning
  • Ask for a written checklist if available
